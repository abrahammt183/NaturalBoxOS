# G-003 — BoxID

## Definition

A **BoxID** is the identity of a Box.

A BoxID consists of the following four fields:

- Name
- Author
- Architecture
- Version

These fields are defined in the Box's `boxInfo`.

## Purpose

A BoxID identifies a Box and distinguishes it from other Boxes.

BoxMaker and BoxKeeper use the BoxID to locate, create, verify, and maintain Boxes.

## Related Terms

- G-001 — Box
- G-002 — boxInfo
- G-004 — BoxType

## Notes (Informative)

A BoxID is defined by the contents of a Box's `boxInfo`.

Changing any field of a BoxID changes the identity of the Box.
